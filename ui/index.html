    Operable-proxy intensity caveat — screening landbase, not a THLB

    The intensity screen uses the SCANFI operable merchantable-forest proxy, not a legal THLB. 150 Mm³ fails the 1.0 m³/ha/yr per-province screen by 16.296 Mm³ and is still 4.492 Mm³ short at 1.2 because documented caps bind; the national operable envelope alone clears at 1.2. 119 Mm³ passes both.
