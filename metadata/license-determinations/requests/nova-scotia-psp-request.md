## Email text

Hello,

I am writing on behalf of CLEWs-C2070, a research project funded by Natural Resources Canada's Emerging Innovators Program (EIP-NES-147). The contract is held by Simon Fraser University, and UBC FRESH is a sub-grantee. I am the PI for this data request. We are building a national forest growth-and-yield layer for an energy-land-water model and request access to Nova Scotia's permanent sample plot (PSP) data under a data-sharing agreement. If this message has reached the wrong office, please forward it to the responsible forestry contact for PSP data.

**Purpose.** The project already holds the open MAGPlot v1.2 Tier-1 package (39,130 sites; 1.57 million remeasured trees), the BC FAIB ground-plot compilations (7,341 PSP sites and 9,512 non-PSP sites), the Québec PEP (12,834 plots), and the NL FAL PSP (1,380 plots). The open MAGPlot package contains no Nova Scotia records. The NL FAL data are our current Atlantic basis, but they cover Newfoundland and Labrador only. Nova Scotia would add the Atlantic Maritime component that is otherwise missing from the national layer. We would use the plots to calibrate species-specific growth, mortality, and site-index relationships, and to test them against held-out plots. This is non-commercial calibration and validation.

**Data requested.** Our inventory records approximately 3,368 Nova Scotia PSP sites measured between 1965 and 2022; please confirm the current counts and coverage. We request, for all available remeasurement cycles:

- plot identifier, coordinates, ownership or landbase, and visit dates;
- plot design: BAF or fixed radius, plot area, and expansion factors;
- tree-level records: persistent tree ID, species, DBH, height, live/dead status, crown class, damage and insect or disease codes, ingrowth, and removals;
- site index with base age, stand age, ecosite or ecological site, soil texture, and drainage;
- disturbance and treatment history, including fire, harvest, and insect outbreaks;
- a data dictionary and location-quality flags.

**Format and use.** CSV or Parquet with a data dictionary; coordinates in NAD83. We request the data under Nova Scotia's data-sharing agreement. We will use them only for this project, publish derived products only, and not redistribute raw records or exact coordinates. Data will be stored on secure project infrastructure with access limited to the named team, retained for the project term, and then destroyed or returned as the agreement requires. Nova Scotia will be acknowledged in publications and derived products. We are not asking to visit sites.

**Next steps.** Please send the current DSA template, any fee schedule, and the expected delivery format and timeline, or direct us to the correct office.
